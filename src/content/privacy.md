## Browsing This Site

This version does not include analytics, advertising trackers, or embedded social-media widgets. Images, scripts, and styles are served with the site. A hosting provider may process standard request information when the site is published.

## Emailing Us

Contact and update-request links open your email application. Sending an email shares its contents and your email address with SoCal Robotics and the email providers involved. Please do not include sensitive student records, financial documents, or payment details.

An update request is not an automatic subscription. We will confirm the request before adding you to an email list. You can contact us to ask to stop receiving updates.

## External Services

If you follow an external link, that service’s own privacy practices apply. This notice will be updated before online donations, signup forms, or other data-collection services are activated.

## Questions

Contact [socalrobotics@gmail.com](mailto:socalrobotics@gmail.com) with privacy questions or requests concerning information you have shared.
