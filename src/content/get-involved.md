## Donate

Your contribution can help provide equipment, materials, programs, competition fees, and other hands-on learning experiences. You help students pursue opportunities that financial barriers might otherwise put out of reach.

[Explore ways to give →](/donate/)

## Corporate Partnerships

Technology companies, engineering firms, manufacturers, and local businesses can help students take their next step. We welcome conversations about:

- **Financial support** for learning opportunities, student teams, or program needs.
- **Equipment and materials** that match an identified educational need.
- **Employee engagement** through expertise, introductions, and potential volunteer opportunities.
- **Connections** to educators, technology programs, and community partners.

We will work with you to understand your interests and discuss a useful fit. Sponsorship arrangements and any recognition should be agreed together; no preset benefits are promised here.

[Start a partnership conversation →](mailto:socalrobotics@gmail.com?subject=Corporate%20partnership%20inquiry)

## Volunteer

Students benefit from meeting people who build, code, design, and solve problems for a living. As opportunities develop, we welcome interest from engineers, programmers, designers, makers, educators, and other community members.

Tell us about your experience, interests, and availability. We will discuss suitable opportunities and any requirements before arranging work with students.

[Ask about volunteering →](mailto:socalrobotics@gmail.com?subject=Volunteer%20inquiry)

## Other Ways to Help

Introduce us to a school. Share a student’s story. Connect us with a potential sponsor. Ask whether your employer offers matching gifts. Let a family know that support may be available.

Have equipment to contribute? Contact us before sending items so we can check whether they meet a current need.

[Make a connection →](mailto:socalrobotics@gmail.com?subject=I%20would%20like%20to%20help)
