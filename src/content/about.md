## Our Mission

Our mission is to expand access to technology education by removing financial barriers for middle school and high school students. We provide funding and support for the tools, programs, competitions, and learning opportunities that help young people explore technology, develop practical skills, and build confidence in their ability to create the future.

## Our Story

SoCal Robotics began with the need to help Team APEX continue competing and volunteering as the students progressed from middle school to high school. Their experience shows both the power of hands-on learning and the costs that can stand in its way.

Our mission reaches beyond robotics. Coding, AI, electronics, engineering, design, prototyping, competitions, and specialized programs can all give young people a chance to turn curiosity into capability.

## Our Impact

Team APEX offers a first window into the experiences we support: designing and coding competition robots, collaborating under pressure, and helping younger students learn.

## Financial Transparency

SoCal Robotics Inc. is identified in its supplied sponsorship material as a California 501(c)(3) nonprofit corporation and public charity. Our EIN is **42-3874219**.

For organizational documentation, donation questions, or information about the use of funds, please contact us. Financial reports and governing documents will be linked here when they are available for publication.
