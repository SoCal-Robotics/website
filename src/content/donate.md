## Invest in a Learning Opportunity

Equipment to build something. Materials to test an idea. Registration for a program or competition. Travel that lets a student participate in an opportunity they have worked toward.

Your support helps remove the financial barriers around these experiences, giving middle school and high school students more room to learn, collaborate, and grow.

## One Opportunity Can Reach Further

Team APEX students design, build, code, and compete. They also return to their former middle school to work with younger teams and volunteer at competitions. Knowledge travels further when students share it.

Contact us about an individual donation, a corporate contribution, an employer matching gift, or equipment that could meet a student need. We will confirm the appropriate giving method and details directly.
