## What We Fund

We support opportunities that let students build, experiment, collaborate, and solve real problems. Support may help with the tools, programs, competitions, and learning experiences that would otherwise be out of reach.

### Robotics & engineering

Components, equipment, tools, and materials that help students design, build, test, and improve their ideas.

### Coding, computer science & AI

Hardware, software, development tools, courses, and learning resources that help students explore programming and artificial intelligence through practical projects.

### Electronics, design & prototyping

Microcontrollers, circuits, fabrication tools, 3D printing, and materials for turning an idea into a working prototype.

### Competitions & specialized programs

Registration fees, educational workshops, camps, and reasonable travel costs associated with hands-on learning and technology competitions.

These are areas of support, not a guarantee that every expense or request can be funded. Contact us about your particular opportunity.

## Why Hands-on Learning Matters

A textbook can explain engineering. Building a robot asks a student to think like an engineer: test an idea, learn from what fails, and try again.

Along the way, students practice creativity, persistence, teamwork, communication, and leadership. Those skills travel with them long after a project or competition ends.

Our focus is middle school and high school students. Formal application details are being developed; you can contact us now to discuss a potential request.
