## From Curiosity to Competition

Team APEX 471A is a student robotics team from Los Angeles. In May 2026, the team won the VEX Robotics World Championship in the middle school division. The team’s sponsorship material describes its three members continuing into ninth grade at North Hollywood High School for the 2026–2027 season.

Behind that moment are the hands-on skills that technology education can build. APEX students design, build, and code competition robots, test their ideas, and make decisions together under tournament pressure.

## The Learning Behind the Robot

A competition robot is a work in progress. Students have to connect mechanical design with code, respond to challenges, and collaborate with teammates and other teams. That experience builds more than technical skill: it asks for persistence, communication, and confidence.

The team’s sponsorship material identifies equipment, tools, tournament travel, and registration fees as costs of participation. These are the kinds of financial barriers SoCal Robotics exists to help address.

## Passing It Forward

APEX students stay connected to their former Title I LAUSD middle school. They run scrimmages with younger teams, help students navigate tournaments, and volunteer at the school’s competitions.

Their experience shows how one learning opportunity can extend beyond a single team. Students who develop skills can share them—and help the next group start further ahead.
