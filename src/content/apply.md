## Who Our Mission Serves

We focus on middle school and high school students pursuing meaningful, hands-on experiences in technology. These can include robotics, engineering, computer science, AI, electronics, design, and related fields.

## Tell Us About the Opportunity

To start a conversation, a parent, guardian, educator, coach, or team representative can share:

1. **The learning experience.** What will the students build, learn, or do?
2. **Who will participate.** A general description of the students and their grade levels is enough for an initial inquiry.
3. **The costs.** An estimated budget for equipment, materials, registration, a program, or travel.
4. **The timing.** Relevant dates and any upcoming deadlines.
5. **The funding gap.** What other support is available, and what is still needed?

Please do not send student identification documents, financial records, or sensitive personal information in an initial email. Students should involve a parent, guardian, or educator.

## What Happens Next

Contact us to discuss whether the opportunity fits our mission and what information may be needed next. Support depends on available resources; an inquiry does not guarantee funding.

Eligibility rules, application deadlines, funding limits, and a formal review process will be published here once they are finalized. No open application round or award amount is being advertised at this stage.
